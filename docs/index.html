Frequently Asked Questions

How can I access past papers?

Past papers are available in our dedicated section. Simply navigate to "Past Papers" in the menu, select your exam board (IGCSE or IAL), then browse by subject to find the papers you need.

Do you offer resources for subjects other than sciences?

Absolutely! While we started with science subjects, we now offer resources for a wide range of subjects including Mathematics, English, History, Geography, Economics, and more. We're constantly expanding our library.
